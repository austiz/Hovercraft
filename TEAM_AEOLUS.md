# Team Aeolus: name, dance, song, uniforms

Chat log from 2026-10-08. This is the team-spirit side of the build: the craft's name,
a dance, a team song with guitar chords, and uniforms. Nothing in here changes the parts.

## "Is olympiad Greek?"

Yes. "Olympiad" comes from the Greek **Ὀλυμπιάς (Olympiás)**, which reached English through
Latin *Olympias* and Old French *olimpiade*.

- **Original meaning:** the four-year period between one Olympic Games and the next. Ancient
  Greeks used olympiads to count years, so a date might be "the 2nd year of the 80th Olympiad."
- **Root:** **Olympia**, the sanctuary of Zeus in Elis where the ancient Games were held. It was
  named after Mount **Olympus**, home of the gods.
- **Modern meaning:** after the Olympic Games were revived in 1896, "Olympiad" also came to mean
  the Games themselves. From there it spread to any big competition, like the Math Olympiad, the
  Chess Olympiad and Science Olympiad (started in the US in 1984).

The "-ad" ending is the same Greek suffix (*-ás, -ádos*) that shows up in "decade," "myriad" and
"Iliad."

## "What's a cool name for this build? Greek mythos"

Names that fit a craft that rides on a cushion of air:

**Best fits**
- **Aeolus**: the keeper of the winds. He gave Odysseus a leather bag with all the winds sealed
  inside. The skirt is basically a bag of wind, so this one fits the build best.
- **Zephyr** (Zephyros): the west wind, the gentle one. A good name for something that glides.
- **Talaria**: the winged sandals of Hermes. A sandal that skims just above the ground is pretty
  much what a hover racer is.

**Speed names**
- **Hermes**: messenger of the gods and the fastest of them.
- **Atalanta**: the huntress no man could outrun. She lost one race only because she kept stopping
  to pick up golden apples.
- **Arion**: an immortal horse, faster than any other.

**Names to skip**
- **Icarus**: he flew, then crashed.
- **Nike**: the name is fine, but it's a brand.
- **Charon**: he does glide over water, but he's the ferryman of the dead.

Pick: **Aeolus**, because the bag of winds matches how the craft works. Team chant:
"Release the winds."

## "Give me a team dance"

### The Aeolus: Release the Winds

Four 8-counts (about 15 seconds). Do it in a line or a V. The person in the middle plays
**Aeolus**.

**Counts 1–8: The Bag**
- **1–4:** Everyone crouches with arms curled in front, like hugging a big sack.
- **5–8:** Puff up slowly as you stand, arms spreading wider, like the skirt inflating.

**Counts 9–16: The Release**
- **9–10:** Aeolus throws both arms up and yells **"RELEASE!"**
- **11–12:** Everyone else shouts **"THE WINDS!"** and flings their hands out.
- **13–16:** Spin once in place with arms out, like you're the fan.

**Counts 17–24: The Hover**
- **17–24:** Shuffle sideways on your toes with knees soft and arms out flat, like you're gliding.
  The line ripples: each person starts one count after the person before.

**Counts 25–32: The Finish**
- **25–28:** Everyone moves into a tight line behind Aeolus.
- **29–30:** Point forward together, as if aiming down the track.
- **31–32:** Make a **whoosh** sound, then freeze in a pose: one arm forward, one arm back, a lean
  like you're racing.

**Extras**
- Song: anything with a big build and a drop. The drop goes on "RELEASE!"
- Prop: the person playing Aeolus carries a trash bag or pillowcase and "opens" it on count 9.
- At the event: do just counts 9–12 ("RELEASE! THE WINDS!") as a quick chant before your run.

## "How is it going to do 9–10?"

It has no arms, so the craft does its own version of counts 9–10:

- **Count 9:** Whoever is playing Aeolus flips the **switch** instead of raising their arms.
- **Count 10:** The fan spins up, the skirt **puffs out** and the craft lifts off the floor.
  That's its "RELEASE!"
- **Counts 11–12:** The team yells **"THE WINDS!"** while it hovers.

Counts 1–8 (the crouch, then puffing up) are the team acting out what the craft is about to do,
and on count 9 the real one does it.

Keep it on the floor while it's running, and don't hold it up near anyone's face. The cage keeps
fingers out, but there's still a lot of air moving.

Split the team in two: one group sings each line, and the other echoes the parts in parentheses.

## "Choreograph song and dance together and give me guitar"

### Setup
- **Guitarist:** stands to one side and doesn't dance.
- **Aeolus (the craft):** sits on the floor in front of the team, switched off.
- **Switch person:** kneels next to the craft and is the only one who touches it.
- **Everyone else:** one line facing the audience, split into **Group A** (sings the lines) and
  **Group B** (sings the echoes).

### Guitar

**Key of G.** If it's too low for your voices, put a capo on fret 2.

| Chord | Frets (low E → high E) |
|---|---|
| G | 3 2 0 0 0 3 |
| G7 | 3 2 0 0 0 1 |
| C | x 3 2 0 1 0 |
| D | x x 0 2 3 2 |

**Strum:** medium-fast and bouncy, about 110 bpm, pattern **Down, Down-Up, Down, Down-Up**.
If that's hard, four plain down-strums per bar works fine.

Every section uses the same chord pattern:
```
G                          G
Oh when the winds    (oh when the winds)
G                          G
Come rushing out     (come rushing out)
G                                      D
Oh when the winds come rushing   out
G            G7                 C
Aeolus is gonna float right off the floor
     G           D          G
When the winds come rushing out!
```

### Full run-through

**Intro: guitar only, 4 bars of G.** Everyone crouches with arms hugging a big imaginary sack:
the **bag of winds**.

**Chorus**
| Line | Move |
|---|---|
| Oh when the winds *(oh when the winds)* | Group A rises halfway on the line, Group B on the echo |
| Come rushing out *(come rushing out)* | Same again, all the way up to standing |
| Oh when the winds come rushing out | Arms spread wide slowly, like the skirt inflating |
| Aeolus is gonna float right off the floor | Shuffle sideways on your toes, arms flat out like you're gliding |
| When the winds come rushing out! | Everyone points at the craft |

**Verse 1: the skirt**
| Line | Move |
|---|---|
| We built a skirt *(we built a skirt)* | Hug the sack again |
| To hold the wind *(to hold the wind)* | Puff your cheeks and widen your arms |
| Oh we built a skirt to hold the wind | Pat the "bag" twice, like checking it |
| Then we spin the fan and up she goes | **Spin once** in place, arms out like fan blades |
| When the winds come rushing out! | Point at the craft |

**Verse 2: the rules**
| Line | Move |
|---|---|
| It fits the box *(it fits the box)* | Draw a square in the air with both index fingers |
| At forty wide *(at forty wide)* | Hold your hands apart like you're measuring something |
| Oh it fits the box at forty wide | Nod at each other, very serious |
| And your fingers can't get through the guard | Lace your fingers into a grid, then wag one finger "no" |
| When the winds come rushing out! | Point at the craft |

**Verse 3: the race**
| Line | Move |
|---|---|
| We'll glide on by *(we'll glide on by)* | **Ripple glide:** each person starts the sideways shuffle one beat after the person before |
| Across the line *(across the line)* | Keep gliding |
| Oh we'll glide on by across the line | Everyone stops together and faces front |
| Every judge is gonna check the clock | Tap your wrist and lean toward the judges |
| When the winds come rushing out! | Point at the craft |

**Final chorus.** Same moves as the first chorus, with these changes on the last line:
- **Guitarist:** slow down on "When the winds come rushing out," hit one big **G** and let it
  ring, then mute the strings.
- **Team:** crouch back into the bag pose and stay silent for 2 seconds.

**The finish**
| Beat | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Switch person | Shouts **"RELEASE..."** |
| 2 | Switch person | **Flips the switch**. The skirt puffs and the craft lifts |
| 3 | Everyone | Jumps up and shouts **"...THE WINDS!"** with hands flung out |
| 4 | Everyone | **Whoosh**, then freeze in the race pose: one arm forward, one arm back, leaning |

The whole thing runs about 2 minutes. Short version for the event: just the first chorus and the
finish (about 30 seconds).

## "I want full matching uniforms and an hour long intro"

### Uniforms

**Colors:** sky blue, white and gold (the sky, the clouds and Greek gold).

| Item | What to get | DIY tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Shirt** | Sky-blue t-shirt with **AEOLUS** across the chest and a white wind swirl | Iron-on vinyl or fabric paint with a cardboard stencil |
| **Back of shirt** | **"RELEASE THE WINDS"** with each person's name or role (Pilot, Switch, Aeolus) | Same vinyl |
| **Sash** | A gold fabric strip worn over one shoulder, like a toga | Cut it from an old bedsheet or a gold tablecloth |
| **Laurel wreath** | A gold leaf headband, like Greek winners wore | Paper leaves hot-glued onto a plastic headband |
| **Bottoms** | White or navy athletic shorts or pants | Whatever everyone already owns |
| **Shoes** | White sneakers | Optional: blue laces for everyone |

**Safety:** tuck the sash in or take it off when you're near the craft. Loose fabric and a fan
intake don't mix. The wreath is fine.

### The hour-long intro

| Time | Act |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Guitar plays four bars of G |
| 0:01 | Guitar plays four more bars of G |
| 0:02–0:45 | The complete Odyssey, acted out, with Aeolus showing up in Book 10 |
| 0:45 | Odysseus's crew opens the bag of winds early (boo) |
| 0:50 | A dramatic reading of `hovercraft_parts.scad`, all 2,400 lines |
| 0:58 | The judges leave |
| 0:59 | Team crouches into the bag pose |
| 1:00 | **"RELEASE... THE WINDS!"** |

Realistically, the event slot is short and the judges are on a schedule, so expect a minute or
less. Use the 30-second version (first chorus plus the finish). Check with the coach about whether
there's any time for it at all, or whether it's better done in the hallway before check-in.
